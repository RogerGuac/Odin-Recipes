High-Protein with Egg White Oatmeal

Ingredients

Oatmeal

  • 1/2 cup of Base Oatmeal
  • 1/2 cup Milk(any time of milk)
  • 1/2 cup Water
  • 1/4 tsp Cinnamon
  • 1 pinch Salt
  • 1 tsp Vanilla extract

Proteins

  • 1/3 cup of Egg Whites
  • 2 tbsp of Peanut butter or almond butter(for less calories)

Optional Toppings

  • 1/2 cup Berries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tsp Honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Chia seeds or flax seeds

Instructions step by step

  1. Combine Oatmeal ingredients: Add the oats, milk, water, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt to a small saucepan.
  2. Simmer the oats: In the small saucepan cook over medium heat for 3 or 4 minutes,stir frequently, until the oats soften and most of the liquid is absorbed.
  3. Turn down the heat: Lower the heat to low to ensure that eggs whites are cooked gently and blend well with oats.
  4. Whisk egg whites: Pour in egg whites while whisking. Keep stirring for the next 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Serve: Once the oats and eggs a cooked through, pull it off the heat. Pour into a bowl and add all your optional toppings
